Backend Engineer (Platform) — Aperture Cloud

About the role
We're hiring a Backend Engineer to design and operate the APIs and services that
power our developer platform. You'll work on high-throughput, low-latency systems
and own features from design through production.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate scalable backend services and RESTful APIs.
- Improve reliability and performance of distributed systems under heavy load.
- Build event-driven pipelines and asynchronous processing workflows.
- Add observability (metrics, logging, tracing) and participate in on-call.
- Collaborate on API design and mentor other engineers through code review.

Requirements
- 4+ years building backend services in Python (Go is a plus).
- Strong experience with FastAPI or similar async web frameworks.
- Solid knowledge of PostgreSQL and caching (Redis).
- Experience with message queues such as Kafka and containerization with Docker.
- Familiarity with AWS and Kubernetes.
- Track record of designing reliable, observable, high-throughput systems.

Nice to have
- Experience with payments or financial systems.
- Open-source contributions.
- gRPC and CI/CD pipeline experience.
